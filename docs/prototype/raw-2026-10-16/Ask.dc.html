On 2 October the backend sync agreed that the drawer listens to the notification service and the direct websocket is left alone1. Today's planning call reaffirmed that2 and added a second decision: backend builds a replay buffer for reconnects in 8.0, and the drawer shows a "reconnecting" state and refetches on recovery3.

Still open: who owns the acceptance criteria for the reconnecting state4, and whether the replay buffer lands in 8.0 or 8.1, which Pavel wanted to confirm with the backend team5. The Confluence drawer spec, section 3, still describes the old direct-websocket design and has not been updated6.
