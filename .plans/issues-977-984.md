## Acceptance and definition of done

- Topics-only empty world is observable on the real core; seeded similar
  centroids yield suggestions, Merge and Keep Separate work through real APIs.
- Search returns its existing core query-language estimate and stored item kind;
  unknown values stay absent. UI uses only returned language/kind information.
- Every new Ask turn retains its own scope after follow-ups and reopening; old
  turns with no recorded scope stay unknown rather than inheriting a later scope.
- Citations carry the stored evidence language, nullable when unknown; quote lang
  reflects it without assuming the interface language.
- Answer meta reports actual retrieved evidence counts and measured elapsed time;
  old answers with no measurement omit the line. Helpful/Not helpful/unset persist
  through reload and restart; failures preserve the last acknowledged value.
- People shows the opted-in date, available profiles and distinct matched calls
  from actual rows, with no biometric payloads or fabricated history.
- Unit regressions, protocol conformance and real-core E2E cover the existing
  cpt-noter-seq-library-follow-topic, cpt-noter-seq-library-search-calls,
  cpt-noter-seq-ask-cited-answer, cpt-noter-seq-ask-follow-up-and-copy and
  cpt-noter-seq-dialogs-set-up-voice-profile stories.
- just gate-fast and just gate pass; local PR and ponytail reviews have no
  blocking findings and just review-publish succeeds. Owner approval remains a
  blocker if config-guard requires it; thresholds and baselines are unchanged.
